About

Portrait

"Paper is the most honest material —
it shows every decision, every hesitation,
every moment of clarity."

ODER × ReDo is the studio of Tim — artist, maker, and educator working in paper. The practice encompasses lampshades and lighting objects, sculptural paper works, and educational offerings for those who want to work with the material themselves.

Paper is the constant. Everything else — form, function, audience — changes around it. The studio takes commissions from private collectors, works with interior designers and architects on project-specific objects, and runs workshops for groups of all sizes and experience levels.

Clients come through Instagram, through designers, and through people who have seen the work in spaces. Each piece is made by hand. Each conversation about a commission starts with a description of the space it will live in.

The practice
has three parts.

01

Art & Objects

Original paper works — lampshades, wall pieces, sculptural objects — made for specific spaces and specific people. Available for acquisition or commission.

02

Education

Workshops, PDF guides, and DIY kits for those who want to make things with paper themselves. From two-hour introductions to half-day lampshade sessions.

03

Collaboration

Project-based work with designers, architects, and institutions. Paper installations, exhibition pieces, and custom objects made at scale.

Studio — process

The material

Paper remembers
every fold.

The work begins with a sheet of paper and a decision about where to put the first fold. Everything follows from that. Paper is unforgiving — it records pressure, hesitation, speed. That honesty is what makes it interesting to work with.

Studio — making

The process

Made slowly,
by hand.

Nothing is produced in batches. Each lampshade, each object, each wall work is made one at a time. The pace of the work is part of what the work is about.
