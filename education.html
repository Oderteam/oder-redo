Education

Learn to fold, in the studio.

Three ways to learn pleating. Take the course, book a private session for a group of any size, or drop into an open workshop. Every format ends with a piece you made yourself.

01

Art and Design in Paper Folding

Academic Course · For Schools & Institutions

A full course held in the studio, built around the pleating technique from first principles: scoring, folding, and shaping a finished piece over several sessions. Structured for schools and academies, ending with a piece you can read and repeat on your own.

Small groups · Multi-session
02

Private Sessions

For Corporate Workshops & Private Events

A session booked privately for a group of any size, from a corporate team of thirty to four people around a table at home. The pace, project, and location are set around the group.

4–30 people · Private booking
03

Open Workshop

Standard Open Workshop

A single drop-in session at a date and place set by the studio. Open to anyone, no experience needed, just an afternoon to learn the fold and leave with something made by hand.

Open signup · Single session
